Title: Real-Time Pilgrim Crowd Density Estimation and Flow Optimization in the Grand Mosque Using Edge-Based Deep Learning

Abstract:
Managing massive pilgrim crowds during Hajj and Umrah is a high-priority challenge for Saudi authorities in Makkah. This research paper presents an advanced, high-precision crowd management system utilizing deep convolutional neural networks (CNNs) deployed on edge-computing devices. We propose a custom multi-column neural network architecture optimized for dense and highly congested environments (such as the Tawaf area and Mina pedestrian pathways). 

The system takes real-time video feeds from existing surveillance infrastructure, processes it locally to preserve privacy and minimize latency, and outputs highly accurate density maps, crowd counting estimations, and directional velocity vectors. Furthermore, a predictive congestion routing algorithm is integrated to simulate crowd flow and automatically suggest alternative routes to crowd control officers before physical bottlenecks form. 

Our experimental prototype has been successfully validated in a simulated laboratory environment using historical multi-spectral video datasets of the Holy Sites, achieving a 94.2% crowd estimation accuracy under extreme density scenarios (above 6 pilgrims per square meter). The proposed solution provides an actionable blueprint for smart city integration under Saudi Vision 2030, enhancing pilgrim safety and optimizing spatial utilization during peak seasons.
